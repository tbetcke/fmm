# CH3.4a The vectorised sine and cosine

Started 2026-11-01 on `tbetcke/h-ch3-4a`. Roadmap: "CH3.4 Helmholtz P2P, real and complex
k" (H3; eleventh on the priority list), split on 2026-11-01 into CH3.4a (this task), CH3.4b
(the kernel) and CH3.4c (the operator's P2P). Proposal: §10 CH3.4; §2 (constraint 1), §5.7,
§6.1, §6.2.

## Goal

sin θ and cos θ together, in `nd-fmm-simd`'s vector layer on every ISA and in both
precisions, with an accuracy contract checked on real hardware and a public slice function
for tests and reports. It is CH3.4b's e^{i Re(k) r}, as CH2.4a's exponential was CH2.4b's
e^{−κr}.

## Acceptance (proposal §10, verbatim)

CH3.4's, of which this sub-task prepares the first item:

> accuracy contract over k r up to 22 c_leaf; cost per pair

CH3.4a's own *Done when* (the split, roadmap):

- sin θ and cos θ each within 4 u_T **absolute** for |θ| ≤ θ_max = 2¹⁰ (above 22 c_leaf for
  any c_leaf ≤ 46; the P2P term's error is measured relative to |q| e^{−Im(k) r}/r, so the
  absolute error of e^{iθ} is the quantity that matters): f32 exhaustively over [0, 2¹⁰],
  f64 on 10⁷ seeded samples against a double-double reference, on every ISA a machine
  offers;
- odd and even bit for bit: sin(−θ) = −sin θ and cos(−θ) = cos θ for every tested θ;
- every ISA equal to the scalar layer bit for bit;
- each value's result independent of its position and of the slice length;
- no call in the inner loop of any entry point (the release disassembly), and its
  instruction count reported;
- Laplace untouched: every existing entry point of the crate the same instruction for
  instruction as `main`'s on aarch64 and x86_64, at the default settings and with one
  codegen unit (`fmm-simd/CLAUDE.md`).

## Changes

`nd-fmm-simd` only, plus docs. No manifest, dependency, convention, `nd-fmm-plan` or layer
change: the formulation needs only the existing `Simd` methods (`fma`, `fnma`, `sub`, `mul`).

- `src/arch/sincos.rs` (new): `sincos(s, θ) -> (sin, cos)`, a free function over `Simd`
  like `exp`, and `sincos_body`, the slice loop (whole vectors in place, the tail in a
  padded copy, as `exp_body`):

  ```text
  k = fma(θ, 1/π, c)                c = 1.5 · 2^m: k = c + n, n = round(θ / π)
  n = k − c                         exact
  t = fnma(n, π₁, θ)                exact (π₁ with trailing zeros: n π₁ exact, Sterbenz)
  t = fnma(n, π₂, t)                exact or rounded once (π₂ likewise)
  t = fnma(n, π₃, t)                |t| ≤ π/2 (+ rounding)
                                    (as built: two parts, π₂ = π − π₁ rounded; "Findings")
  s = t · t
  S = fma(t s, P(s), t)             sin t, Taylor to t^(2N_s+1) by Horner in s
  C = Q(s)                          cos t, Taylor to t^(2N_c) by Horner in s
  h = round(n / 2) (as k);  b = n − 2h ∈ {−1, 0, 1};  σ = 1 − 2 b²     σ = (−1)ⁿ, exact
  sin θ = σ S,  cos θ = σ C         exact
  ```

  Taylor degrees: f64 sin to t²¹ and cos to t²⁰, f32 sin to t¹³ and cos to t¹² (truncation
  at most about 0.2 u_T at |t| = π/2; minimax coefficients of lower degree only if the
  measurement leaves the f64 bound tight, generated by a committed script). The sign by
  exact arithmetic keeps the layer unchanged; an integer sign flip (a new layer method like
  `scale_pow2`) is the fallback if the instruction count shows the four operations matter
  in CH3.4b.
- Constants through `SimdScalar`'s sealed trait (`Sealed::SINCOS: SincosConstants`, as
  `EXP`); `src/sincos.rs` (new, public): `sincos_slice(isa, θ, sin, cos)`, the analogue of
  `exp_slice`, with the table of formulations and the contract; entry points
  `sincos_slice_f32`/`_f64` per ISA, `#[inline(never)]`, a dispatch macro
  `sincos_dispatch!` as `exp_dispatch!`.
- `src/lib.rs`: the module, the crate docs' table, `Sealed::sincos_slice`.
- docs/design/simd-p2p.md: a new §4.11 "The sine and cosine (Helmholtz CH3.4a)": the
  formulation, the derivation behind 4 u_T, the domain, the measured errors, the operation
  count. `fmm-simd/CLAUDE.md`: the public module, its contract, its entry points in the
  inlining check.

**Domain.** |θ| ≤ 2¹⁰: |n| ≤ 326 (9 bits), so π₁ and π₂ carry 15 significant bits in f32
(44 in f64) and n π₁, n π₂ are exact; the shifter needs |n| < 2^(m−1). Beyond 2¹⁰ and for
non-finite θ the result is unspecified (the P2P never forms such a phase from the leaf
rule; CH3.4b states what its entry point guarantees there).

**Expected error** (to be derived in §4.11): the reduced t carries one rounding, at most
½ ulp(π/2) = u_T absolute; Horner adds about 1.6 u_T (cos at |t| near π/2, where its
terms of size 1.2 cancel); the rest is below 0.2 u_T. A prototype in f32 (exact fma, 3 · 10⁵
points over [0, 2¹⁰] and the multiples of π/2) measured 1.85 u (sin) and 3.14 u (cos), the
worst at θ ≈ 834 with |t| ≈ 1.42. So 4 u_T holds with about 20 % margin in f32; the f64
margin is measured.

## Tests that prove it

All on every ISA the machine offers (`common::isas`, printing those it cannot run);
`tests/sincos/` (`main.rs`, `common.rs`, `contract.rs`, `invariance.rs`), shaped as
`tests/exp/`.

- **Contract, f32:** every f32 in [0, 2¹⁰] (about 1.15 · 10⁹ values; `#[ignore]`,
  release) against `f64::sin`/`f64::cos` of the same value (error far below u₃₂), within
  4 u₃₂ absolute each; the debug run a strided subset.
- **Contract, f64:** 10⁷ seeded samples (`#[ignore]`, release; 10⁵ in debug), half uniform
  on [0, 2¹⁰] and half 2^e with e uniform in [−30, 10), against a double-double oracle in
  the test (n π subtracted with π in three doubles, Taylor in double-double), within 4 u₆₄
  absolute. The oracle is checked against 40-digit values computed once with mpmath and
  written into the test, and against `f64::sin`/`cos` to 1 u.
- **Special inputs:** ±0 (sin ±0 with its sign, cos exactly 1); the reduction's boundaries
  θ = (n + ½) π for every n of the domain and their neighbours; the multiples n π (sin
  near 0, the absolute contract); θ_max and its neighbours.
- **Symmetry:** sin(−θ) = −sin θ and cos(−θ) = cos θ bit for bit over the samples and,
  in the exhaustive run, over every f32 of the domain.
- **Invariance:** the result of each value the same at every position and slice length,
  bit for bit; every ISA equal to the scalar ISA bit for bit (as `tests/exp/invariance.rs`).
- **Laplace:** the whole existing suite unchanged (no existing test edited).

## Constraints and risks

- Constraint 1 applies to `nd-fmm-simd` as a whole, but CH3.4a only adds a module, a sealed
  constant, a sealed method and entry points; no existing method, body or entry point
  changes, so Laplace's bits cannot change. Adding items can shift rustc's codegen-unit
  partition (CH2.4a's `sums_f32`); the comparison at both settings shows it, and a shift
  that reaches a Laplace entry point at the default settings is timed by D9's protocol, as
  CH2.4a timed `sums`.
- Risk: the cosine near |t| = π/2 in f64. If the f64 measurement comes near 4 u, the
  fallback is the quadrant reduction by π/2 (|t| ≤ π/4, shorter and better-conditioned
  polynomials) with the swap formed by exact arithmetic (products by 0 and ±1).
- Risk: AVX2 has no 64-bit integer conversion; the shifter constant avoids it, as in the
  exponential.

## Findings during implementation

- **Two parts suffice.** t = fnma(n, π₁, θ) is exact (Sterbenz), and one more fnma with
  π₂ = π − π₁ rounded to the precision rounds once; the third part of the plan would only
  add a second rounding. |n| |π − π₁ − π₂| is below 10⁻²⁸ (f64) and 0.012 u₃₂ (f32). One
  operation fewer than planned: 25 (f32) and 33 (f64) per value.
- **sin(−0) = +0.** The sine's last step, fma(t s, P(s), t), adds the correction t s P(s)
  to t; at t = −0 that correction is +0 (P(0) = −1/6), and −0 + +0 = +0. Any form t + w
  with w ≤ 0 does the same; keeping −0 needs t (1 + s P(s)), one more rounding of the order
  of u_T sin t. The P2P's phase Re(k) r is never negative, so the sign of that zero does not
  matter there: the symmetry holds bit for bit for θ ≠ 0, and the plan's "sin ±0 with its
  sign" is not met (question 1 below; the roadmap's *Done when* says "as built").
- **The bound.** Derived to first order, 2.7 u (sine) and 3.4 u (cosine) in both precisions
  (`fmm-simd/tools/sincos_bound.py`; design §4.11); measured 1.89 and 2.57 u.
- **Taylor suffices.** No minimax coefficients were needed (no script beyond the bound's).
