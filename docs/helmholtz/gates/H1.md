# H1 gate: conventions, math, reference

Date: 2026-11-19. Milestone H1 of `docs/helmholtz/roadmap.md` (GitHub issue #141). The
proposal `docs/design/helmholtz-fmm-proposal.md` (draft 0.8) covers it in §10 H1, §4, §9,
§9.2 and §12 D2 and D5. This report was written on branch `tbetcke/h-h1-gate` from `main` at
8475009 (#229).

This is the third Helmholtz gate, after H3 (`gates/H3.md`) and H6 (`gates/H6.md`). It was off
the priority list and became runnable when #229 merged. Its review is
`docs/reviews/2026-11-19-H1.md`.

## Summary

- **The gate condition is met.** The roadmap asks for "CONVENTIONS §3.15 signed off;
  `nd-fmm-math` and `nd-fmm-ref` carry the Helmholtz family, checked against FMM3D's fixtures".
  - §3.15 was signed off at version 3 on 2026-10-29 (CH1.1). Its revision, version 4, was
    signed off on 2026-11-19 (CH1.3d2, #229).
  - `nd-fmm-math` has `orthonormal`, `radial` and `coaxial` (CH1.2a–c). `nd-fmm-ref` has the
    direct sum, the leaf operators, RCR and direct translations, and the diagonal form in
    extended precision (CH1.3a–d1).
  - The direct sum matches FMM3D's `h3ddirectcg` fixtures to 9.9·10⁻¹⁶ relative ℓ2 (CH1.3a).
    Every operator above it is tested against that direct sum.
- **All 10 tasks are done:** CH1.4, CH1.1, CH1.2a–c, CH1.3a–c, CH1.3d1 and CH1.3d2 (#133, #135,
  #136, #137, #149, #150, #151, #152, #228, #229). Each was merged with its record or report
  and a progress entry. No task has a pending run, and every question they raised is signed
  off.
- **Laplace was not touched** by any H1 task. Two version bumps (3, 4) made Laplace's cached
  tables and tuning files rebuild once, and the rebuilt tables are identical.
- **CI on `main`.** Every H1 merge passed every job. In the period two `run-tests` runs were
  cancelled at the then 15-minute limit, on #131 (CH0.8a) and #138 (the issue sync). Neither
  was an H1 merge and no test failed. The limit was raised to 30 minutes on 2026-10-31 and to
  45 on 2026-11-12.
- **Three gaps in the record, none in the code (§2):**
  - CH1.4's signed-off question 3 was never applied: the proposal's R12 row and §9.2 still
    call FMM3D's accuracy for imaginary zk "not verified".
  - D5's "dipoles get a later, separate task" has no task in the roadmap, while CH9.1 refers
    to "D5's task".
  - CH1.3c's finding on RCR under strong damping is not on CH2.3's line, although its progress
    entry sends it there.
- **The review (§5).** Both passes judged H1's mathematics and code sound. The record pass
  re-derived §3.15 at version 4 in full and found no error. The most consequential finding
  concerns H4 rather than H1: A5 (medium-high, checked): H4's expected gain with the default
  φ step is about 2.3× on a high-frequency level, not the larger figure the record calls
  "likelier".
- **For sign-off (§7):** the gate, four corrections to the proposal and roadmap, a dipole task
  for D5, the review's triage, H4's φ-step order (A5), and a small CI PR.

## 8. Retrospective on the loop

What worked:

- Splitting CH1.2 and CH1.3 into one-spike, one-module PRs. Eight of the ten tasks merged
  within three days, each with a self-review and nothing pending.
- Fixtures from the spikes' mpmath generators, value for value, made each port a test against

- Deferring CH1.3d until CH0.6 had fixed the signatures avoided writing the diagonal form
  twice. Splitting it into code and convention let the convention's text be signed off on its
  own.

What to change:

- **Signed-off "for the proposal's next draft" items get lost (G1).** CH1.4's question 3 asked
  for a later edit and had no owner. `/next-task`, at "log": a signed-off change to the
  proposal is applied in the task's own PR with its Appendix A line, or written into the
  roadmap as an "Also" of a named task. Nothing is left for "the next draft".
- **A decision that promises a task must create it (G2).** When a decision in the table is
  signed off with "a later task", `/next-task` adds that task to the roadmap in the same PR.
  `/milestone-gate` step 2 checks the decision table for promised tasks that do not exist.
- **Follow-ups that name a later task are not carried (G3).** A progress entry's "Follow-ups"
  that names another task's ID becomes an "Also" on that task's roadmap line in the same PR.
- **The gate came three weeks after most of its tasks.** That is right for a gate off the
  list, but the H1 record was cold when checked. No change is proposed: the gate's checks
  found what was missing.
