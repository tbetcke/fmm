# Project review at the H1 gate (2026-11-19)

The second project review, repeated at every milestone gate (`/milestone-gate`, step 3; the
first: `docs/reviews/2026-11-12-H6.md`). There were two independent read-only passes over `main`
at 8475009 (#229, CH1.3d2). Each was run by one subagent on Fable at medium effort, from the
briefs `.claude/skills/milestone-gate/review-record.md` and `review-architecture.md`. Nothing
was compiled, run or changed.

- **Pass 1, the record:** the mathematics, the algorithmic decisions and the benchmarks.
  It started with what the first review had not read, then took H1's record and what changed
  since 4c05636.
- **Pass 2, the architecture:** the crate split, the kernel families, the API, bloat, the
  tests' structure and the build. It started with what the first review had not reached, then
  nd-fmm-math, nd-fmm-ref and the changes since 4c05636.

The driving session checked the findings marked ✔ against the tree or the document they name.
It checked every finding of high or medium-high severity, and every finding on which the
triage rests a correction or a new task. The others stand as the passes reported them.
*Suspicion* marks what a pass could not verify. New findings continue the first review's
letters and numbers. Cite one as "review 2026-11-19, A5".

## 1. Pass 1: mathematics, algorithms, benchmarks

### 1.1 Checked and found sound

Re-derived by hand:

- **CONVENTIONS §3.15 at version 4:**
  - "Level units": the multipoles are unit-free, the locals scale by r, L2L carries ½, P2L
    scales its charges by r_t/r_s, M2P needs no factor, and the factor is 4πik̂_l.
  - The stored leaf operators have non-positive exponents. M2M's and L2L's damping cancels.
    The M2L's scalar e^{2β − Im(k)|t|} ≤ 1.
  - Yukawa: k₀, h_n(ix) = −i⁻ⁿ k_n, and the three real table factors.
  - The coaxial start column.
  - "Exponential M2L": the Sommerfeld form, the factor 1/(iⁿ⁺¹ κ), the constant 2π, waves to
    local, and |s/κ| = √3/2.
  - "Signatures":
    - the identity L = 4πik iⁿ ∫ Y T F with its signs;
    - the exactness of 2p + 1 Gauss rings with N_φ ≥ 4p + 1;
    - the stored chain F̂, T̂, G, L̂;
    - 316 offsets in 34 classes;
    - the four sample maps.
  - §3.14's and §3.15's Fourier counts now agree.
- **The proposal's numbers:** §3.5's plateau and calibrated degrees, §3.4's and §3.6's
  magnitudes, §7's pair and box counts, and §6.7's 141 M per box at p = 144.
- **The spike reports' arithmetic:** S6's grid, pair shares and 5.5 s per box; S7's C(ε) and
  closed form; S2's table size and threshold.
- **CH4.0e's tables:** about 20 ratios and the Kathleen geometric mean (0.61).

Decisions judged well founded:

- the empty-box rule;
- the (parent, source) grouping of V pairs on wave levels;
- the leaf-size defaults (three sweep rounds on two machines);
- the block budget of 512 MiB;
- RCR by pairs by degree;
- ε kept as an ℓ2 tolerance with a 100 ε box bound;
- one shift table for M2M and L2L;
- the 2E reading of CH1.3d1.

CH4.0e's method was also judged sound: one oracle for both codes, matched accuracy, two
launches in opposite order, exclusive nodes, and equal checksums.

### 1.3 Algorithms

- **A5 (medium-high) ✔. H4's expected gain is overstated for the default φ step.**
  - CH4.0e calls S6's conversions "unoptimised" and the estimate with them (6–14 % of build
    plus one evaluation on W1 at kD = 500) "a pessimistic floor"
    (`fmm-validate/results/helmholtz-ch4-0e.md` §1, lines 128–140). It calls the estimate
    from the pairs alone (13–30 %) "the likelier".
  - The record's own counts say otherwise.
    - Proposal §6.7 puts a conversion as a product at about 18 P³ complex multiply-adds
      (2 P³ for Legendre, 16 P³ for φ). §7 puts an RCR pair at 2 P³.
    - So a box's two conversions cost about 18 RCR pairs. S6's measured ratio is the same:
      53 ms per box against 3.0 ms per RCR pair at p = 128, about 17.6
      (`spikes/diagonal/SPIKE_REPORT.md` §8).
    - With about 45 V pairs per box on W1 (§7's table: 46–48), a diagonal level costs about
      36 P³ + 360 P² against RCR's 90 P³. That is about 2.3× cheaper at any kD.
    - §7's own totals agree: on levels 2–5, 28 with the diagonal form against 62 with RCR.
  - The large gain needs the FFT φ step. That is priority item 10 (CH0.9, CHR.3, CH4.6),
    after CH4.1–CH4.5, optional and off by default.
  - The driver's check: the counts hold. CH4.0e's counter-argument is real but unmeasured: a
    level-batched product runs at GEMM rate, which S6's per-ring loop does not. CH4.0e's
    "pessimistic" label rests on that difference.
  - Proposed: CH4.1 first measures the product conversion per box against the RCR pair,
    including the ×4 from the grid's symmetries. The roadmap then states H4's expected gain
    for the product and for an FFT separately.
- **A6 (medium). Three acceptances in a row were met "as read".**
  - CH4.0h: the build was "at most half its one-core time" only at the best split; at one rank
    per node it is 0.44–0.66.
  - CH4.0i: "≥ 70 % of linear" was met against the old one-core baseline.
  - CH4.0j: the target was met on 8 of 51 and 12 of 43 case–ε pairs.
  - Each was disclosed and signed off, and the causes are sound. The residuals CH4.0k, CH4.0l
    and CH4.0f are off the list, and all concern one rank per node.
  - Proposed: the H4 gate states the figures at one rank per node, and CH4.0k is reconsidered
    before CH4.5.
- **A7 (low-medium). The P2P phase at c_leaf = 2 in f32.** With V pairs as near pairs, P2P
  reaches Re(k) r ≤ 88 (`helmholtz-ch4-0d3.md` §4). In f64 that is about 1.2·10⁻¹³ per term. In
  f32 the same contract gives about 6.5·10⁻⁵, against D3's f32 scope of ε ≥ 10⁻⁴. Proposed: the
  f32 phase bound goes in CH7.2's line, as M9 went in CH7.1's.
- **A8 (low). The high-frequency degrees look over-resolved.** On W1 at kD = 1000 and 10⁻⁸,
  levels 2–4 carry 0.6–1.0 % of Σ|e|² (`helmholtz-ch4-0c.md` §2.2). This is M7 seen from the
  other side. Proposed: CH4.3's refit searches downward at x ≥ 54.

### 1.4 Benchmarks

- **B6 (medium) ✔. Every headline against FMM3D is ours at the best of four splits.** One rank
  per node "is never the best", and the worst split takes up to 1.9× (CH4.0e §2.4, lines
  482–485). FMM3D runs one process, so a threaded caller gets 1 × N. On Kathleen at 1 × 40:
  - W3 at kD = 100 and 10⁻³: 0.97 of `fastker`, against 0.50 at the best split;
  - the W4 cube at 10⁻⁶: 1.08, against 0.83.
  - The roadmap line says "each at its best split" ✔. Proposed: CH4.5 and CH9.1 report 1 × N
    beside the best split.
- **B7 (low-medium) ✔. The roadmap quotes locust's flattering variant.** The CH4.0e line has
  "0.27–0.38× on locust", against `fastker`. That variant is anomalously slow on locust
  (report, line 32). Against FMM3D's faster variant the figure is 0.47–0.54 (line 29).
- **B8 (low, suspicion). FMM3D's thread placement.** FMM3D ran OpenMP at its defaults on a
  two-socket node, while ours was bound per socket. The pass found no `OMP_PROC_BIND` or
  `OMP_PLACES`.
- **B9 (low). Small cases compare overheads, not FMMs.** In many matched rows FMM3D's match is
  its direct sum. The 18-case geometric mean weights them equally with the two cases of
  N ≥ 10⁶ (0.18–0.50).
- **B10 (low).** CH1.2c's timings ran at a load average of about 40. This was disclosed, and
  no decision rests on them.

### 1.5 Risks

2. The kD = 1000 gate: the build, projection tables at p ≈ 250, and the trusted path untested
   there (M11).
3. Once H4 lands, the raised-degree low-frequency levels dominate at 10⁻⁷ and 10⁻⁸. On W1 at
   kD = 500 and 10⁻⁸, levels 6 and 5 take 3.1 s of an 11.3 s evaluation, and no listed task
   targets them.
4. One rank per node (A6, B6).
5. Per-box error: the constant density reached 42 ε in the worst box at 10⁻⁸.
6. H2 and H5:
   - Yukawa's degree grows like √(κa) (S3), and damped k needs 2–2.5× the formula (S7).
   - RCR's M2L loses relative accuracy under strong damping (CH1.3c).
   - FMM3D is a reference only for Im kD ≲ 30–50 (CH1.4).
   - The diagonal form is untested for Yukawa and for η > 0.1.
   - M13's overflow.
7. f32 (M9, A7).

### 2.2 Judged sound, to be left alone

- **The graph and the rules.** nd-fmm-math, -ref, -tables, -simd and -kernels are free of MPI,
  the octree and RLST. CubeCL is in nd-fmm-kernels alone. `unsafe` sits where CLAUDE.md says,
  with the lint tables in eight manifests.
- **H1's modules in nd-fmm-math:** small, generic over `RealScalar`, writing into caller
  slices, and sharing `Layout` and the block ranges with Laplace.
- **Yukawa in nd-fmm-ref:** the `_yukawa` twins are thin wrappers over one body
  (`rcr.rs:411-493`; the `Field` trait in `leaf.rs`).
- **nd-fmm-simd's one `Simd` trait,** with bodies written once. Laplace, Yukawa and Helmholtz
  P2P share one skeleton.
- **`driver::HostKernel`, `parts::Parts`,** and the choices made in one place per path
  (`P2pChoice::resolve`, the ISA once, the Helmholtz M2L form per level call).
- **Code markers:** no TODO, `todo!`, `unimplemented!` or `#[deprecated]`, and every `allow`
  carries its reason.
- **The split from the spikes.** The root lock has 548 packages, one RLST and no git source.

### 2.3 Crate structure

- **C4 (medium) ✔. The oracle crate is the production leaf path.**
  - `fmm-exec/src/helmholtz/densities.rs:419-456` calls `nd_fmm_ref::helmholtz::leaf::p2m` (and
    `p2m_yukawa`) once per source with one-element slices, and `l2p` likewise (`:514-521`).
    Laplace's operator imports `nd_fmm_ref::leaf` (`operator.rs:327`).
  - So the rule "every fast path against a slower trusted path" stops at the leaf operators,
    and a change to the oracle moves the FMM's timings.
  - *Suspicion:* the per-call cost is not measured.
- **C5 (medium, before CH4.1) ✔. The 316 offsets and their classes exist in three forms.**
  - `fmm-plan/src/interaction_manager.rs:86` (`V_LIST_DIRECTIONS`);
  - `fmm-tables`' `symmetry` (16 classes under the 48 `SignedPermutation`s);
  - new in H1, `fmm-ref/src/helmholtz/diagonal/classes.rs` (34 classes under flips and the
    x ↔ y swap, with its own `offsets()` and `Symmetry`).
  - CH4.1 has to map one onto another.
- **C6 (low).** Gauss–Legendre by Newton is written four times in H1's sources (`coaxial/
