### 1.2 Mathematics

- **M1 (medium). §3.15 does not describe the stored Helmholtz expansions as built.**
  CONVENTIONS §3.15 ("Radial functions and scaling", "Coaxial translations") says a local
  represents φ̃, M2L applies 4πik, and M2M and L2L apply their tables with s_c and s_P
  alone. As built (`docs/helmholtz/tasks/CH3.1a.md`), a box of level l holds r_l φ̃ in level
  units with k̂_l = k r_l, the factor is 4πik̂_l, the L2L tables carry an extra ½, and P2L
  scales its charges. The level units appear only in the "Exponential M2L" bullet, whose
  constant 2π depends on them. A known deferral (CH3.1a question 2; H3 gate §1.4), but
  CH1.3d adds "Signatures" to the same section.
- **M2 (low) ✔. The proposal contradicts CONVENTIONS on the level scale.** Proposal §3.4:
  s_l = min(|k| r_l, 1) with the half-width; CONVENTIONS §3.15: s_l = min(|k| a_l, 1) with
  the circumradius. Proposal §3.1 also says no operator carries ik; §3.15 puts 4πik on M2L,
  M2P and P2L. CONVENTIONS wins by rule; the proposal has no Appendix A line for it.
- **M3 (low). The versioning rule against "unversioned".** CONVENTIONS says any change to
  §3.15 bumps `CONVENTION_VERSION`; the "Exponential M2L" subsection inside §3.15 declares
  itself unversioned, "as §3.14".
- **M4 (low, suspicion). The Fourier counts of §3.14 and §3.15 read differently.** §3.14:
  orders 0 ≤ m < F_k' = min(F_k, p + 1); §3.15: F_k' = min(F_k, p) with m = 0..F_k'
  inclusive. If F_k means the same in both, Helmholtz keeps one more order. Not checked
  against the code.
- **M5 (medium-high). The degree rule is a fit to a diluted error measure.** The
  calibration (proposal §3.5, CH3.3) and every gate use the relative ℓ2 error over 1,000
  sampled targets, with charges uniform in [−1, 1] and targets equal to sources. With
  random signs the exactly computed near field dominates ‖φ‖ and ‖∇φ‖, so the far field's
  truncation error is diluted (CH3.6 §1 says so for the W4 sphere). The margin is thin: S7's
  per-pair degrees are about twice the plateau (34 against 15 at 10⁻⁶); the worst box
  reaches 57 ε pointwise and 146× the ℓ2 error (CH3.6). No run in the record uses a smooth
  or coherent density (a plane-wave trace, a constant density), the boundary-element case
  the proposal names as the main use. CH4.0b covers the pointwise question, not the charges.
- **M6 (low). The plateau's quadratic form is empirical.** Theory gives p about linear in
  log(1/ε); the measured increments per decade are 2, 3, 3, 5, 4. The rule covers every
  measured degree in 10⁻³…10⁻⁸, but `helmholtz-ch3-3.md` advertises 10⁻¹⁸ ≤ ε ≤ 10⁻¹, which
  only means it stays below S7's closed form.
- **M7 (medium; known as CH4.0c). The high-frequency branch has the wrong ε-dependence.**
  The excess p − x grows about linearly in log(1/ε), not as its 2/3 power (CH3.3 §3); one
  constant over-predicts by 8 degrees at 10⁻³ and leaves 2 at 10⁻⁸ (88 against 86 at
  x = 65); above x = 65 one check, at x = 108; below S7's p* from x ≥ 128. CH4.4 gates at
  kD = 1000.
- **M8 (medium, a suggestion, not verified). The transfer-function length has three tested
  values.** S6 tested L = P − 1, ⌈3(P − 1)/2⌉ and 2P − 2. The excess-bandwidth formula of
  the high-frequency FMM literature, L ≈ 2ka + c (2ka)^{1/3}, lies between the last two. As
  the rounding grows exponentially in L − k|t|, a few degrees fewer could move the nearest
  classes from RCR to the diagonal form at 10⁻⁸.
- **M9 (low; for CH7.1). The f32 range at low frequency.** §3.15 absorbs no double
  factorial, so stored ĵ_n-type quantities carry 1/(2n + 1)!!, about 10⁻⁴¹ at n = 30, below
  f32's normal range; the raised degrees are 28 and 32. The proposal notes the effect at
  n = 60 for f64 only.
- **M10 (editorial).**
  - `laplace-fmm-plan.md` §2.5 attributes 0.76ᵖ = (√3/(4 − √3))ᵖ to the evaluation of a
    multipole expansion; it is the M2L ratio, the evaluation's is √3/3 ≈ 0.58.
  - CONVENTIONS §3.9: f64 tested to p ≤ 30, the irregular harmonics to degree 40 only (M2L
    to p = 20); it does not say that M2L beyond p = 20 is untested.
  - The proposal's header still says "nothing here is signed off" (D0: 2026-10-26).
  - Proposal §3.5's example table has a level 8 the built tree lacks (H3 §3.1).

### 1.3 Algorithms

- **A1 (medium-high). "H4 next" overstates its evidence.** The priority list says the far
  field at kD = 500 "is mostly the high-frequency levels' RCR". For W1 at kD = 500:

  | Source | Levels 2–3 on RCR | M2L | Evaluation |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Kathleen 1 × 40, 10⁻⁶ (`helmholtz-ch6-2b.md`) | 8.0 s | 20.1 s | 23.0 s |
  | locust 1 × 72, 10⁻⁶ (the same report) | 1.9 s | 4.26 s | 5.0 s |
  | Kathleen, 10⁻⁷ and 10⁻⁸ (H6 §8) | about 9 s | 38–41 s | 42–47 s |

  The levels H4 can touch are 35–40 % of an evaluation at 10⁻⁶ and about 20 % at 10⁻⁷ and
  10⁻⁸: the largest single item, not most of it; and about nothing at kD ≤ 100, where most
  of D1 lies (H3 §3.3) and FMM3D's lead was largest. H4 is the largest milestone left.
- **A2 (medium). The width cap c_leaf = 1 forces shallow leaves on surfaces, and CH4.0d
  keeps it.** W1 at kD = 500 has every leaf on level 7 (0.62 λ; level 6 is 1.24 λ):
  1.99·10⁶ points in 74,337 leaves, about 27 per leaf, against the target of 64 and FMM3D's
  300–1,000. Level 7 costs 4.96 s of the 20.1 s of M2L on Kathleen, and P2P a few per cent
  of the run. D11's rule ("no high-frequency leaf") is not a cost criterion, and what
  motivated it has slack (the P2P contract holds to Re(k) r ≤ 2¹⁰; level 6's degree is 16
  against 15). CH4.0d sweeps the points per leaf "with c_leaf kept as the width cap", so it
  cannot change this tree.
- **A3 (medium). The resolving degrees 28 and 32 rest on small cases and one job.** Chosen
  on 26 cases of at most 1.1·10⁵ points; the large cases verified afterwards in one job
  (0.45–0.84× Rotation's errors, fine). Two constants keyed to two decades of ε. The
  remaining risk is M5's: a smooth density may meet the same truncated tail at 10⁻⁶, where
  iprec 2 at p = 15 was accepted on random charges within 1.07×.
- **A4 (low; in the record already).** d_pw unmeasured on AVX2, scalar and one-plane paths
  (H6 §2); the Helmholtz dense budget per rank against constraint 7 (in CH4.0); `PlaneWave`
  in f32 "untested and not refused".

### 1.4 Benchmarks

- **B1 (medium). "22.4 s against FMM3D's 61.6 and 43.2 s" is quoted without its
  qualifier.** Ours is the evaluation, the build apart; FMM3D's is the whole call, a 4.0 s
  setup included. ✔ `helmholtz-ch6-2d.md` says "our build apart", under the heading "The whole
  call is faster in ours"; proposal §7, H6 §1.2 and §3.1 and the roadmap's CH6.2d line drop
  the qualifier. Our build at 10⁻⁶ is not reported there; on this case it took 10.6–14.8 s
  at 10⁻⁷ and 10⁻⁸ (H6 §8). If it is about that at 10⁻⁶, one call is 33–37 s against 43.2 s
  (`fastker`), about 1.2× ahead, not 1.9× (an inference, to be measured in CH4.0e). FMM3D
  is 2–5× more accurate there (φ 6.2e-8 against 1.45e-7, ∇φ 7.2e-8 against 3.7e-7).
- **B2 (medium). No like-for-like comparison with FMM3D under the current default.** CH3.6
  is the only matrix with both codes at the same settings, from before `Auto`, D8 and the
  raised degree. H6 §3.5's "near 0.6 s" and CH4.0d's "1.2–3.9×" are estimates stitched from
  runs at different rank × thread splits. CH4.5 compares on the kD sweep, not on the low-kD
  W4 and W3 cases, where the gap was largest.
- **B3 (disclosed). The same requested ε is not the same accuracy.** FMM3D is 10–100× more
  accurate at 10⁻⁸ and misses ε by 1.2–2.1× at 10⁻³ at kD = 100; the record says so. With
  M5, "ours meets ε at 0.1–0.6 ε" and "FMM3D over-delivers" may be partly one fact.
- **B4 (low). Single launches in CH3.6's tables,** some of 0.02–0.5 s. The CH6.x runs
  (ABBA, checksums) are sound.
- **B5 (low). Kathleen is 3.3–5.4× slower than locust per level on one case** (W1,
  kD = 500, 10⁻⁶: 23.0 s against 5.0 s at a core ratio of 1.8), without a remark in the
  record. Every H4 gate runs on Kathleen.

### 1.5 Risks for H4 and later

1. The degree rule's validity (M5, M7): CH4.3 calibrates C_T, θ and the high-frequency
   degrees on the same random charges.
2. H4's payoff inside D1 (A1), for scratch of 0.3–1.3 GiB per level and a new convention
   section.
3. The tree's shape (A2): CH4.0, CH4.4 and CH4.5 are timed on trees CH4.0d may change.
4. Memory: the default at 10⁻⁷ and 10⁻⁸ peaks at 19–22.5 GB on one rank for 2–4·10⁶ points
   (H6 §8), the waves buffer at its 8 GiB cap; the signature scratch (proposal §7) comes on
   top; CH0.8b, the census that replaces §7, is off the priority list.
5. §3.15's drift (M1) as "Signatures" is added.
6. kD = 1000 (CH4.4) is outside D1 and the degree rule's measured range, on a new form.
7. HR, H0, H1 and H2 are open; Yukawa and the pruning are untested in the FMM, and H5
   needs both.

### 2.2 Judged sound, to be left alone

- The graph is `docs/design/workspace-structure.md` §2's, without cycles, reverse edges or
  feature tricks. nd-fmm-math, -ref, -tables, -simd and -kernels are free of MPI, the octree
  and RLST. CubeCL appears only in nd-fmm-kernels and the spikes.
- `FmmOperator`, `FmmSizes`, `PairOperator`, `PerPair` and `HostData`
  (`fmm-plan/src/operator.rs`): coherent and minimal, three real implementations.
- `driver::HostKernel` (`fmm-exec/src/driver.rs`) and `pipeline.rs`: the right seam between
  Laplace and Helmholtz. Plan and octree were untouched by Helmholtz apart from
  `OctreeOptions::with_min_leaf_level`.
- `Engine { Host, Device(Box<dyn DeviceDriver>) }`: dynamic dispatch once per level call;
  the ISA resolved once; two host instantiations times two scalars.
- The oracles kept by name (`P2pChoice::Reference`, `MultipoleRouting::Direct`,
  `DeviceReduction::Walk`, `PerPair`, the shadow operator); the fixtures.
- Helmholtz's own batched block (`helmholtz/batched.rs`, 169 lines; the user, 2026-11-02).
- No TODO, FIXME, `todo!` or `unimplemented!` in the ten crates; six `dead_code`/`unused`
  allows, each with its reason.
- The exact CubeCL pins; nd-fmm-kernels as a member outside the default members.

- **C2 (medium). nd-fmm-exec holds three products in very large files.** `device.rs` 6,117

### 2.4 The Laplace–Helmholtz seam

- **L1 (high) ✔ in part. Two facades of diverging capability.** `Fmm`/`FmmBuilder`
  (`fmm.rs:1266`, `:3629`) and `HelmholtzFmm`/`HelmholtzFmmBuilder`
  (`helmholtz/fmm.rs:165`, `:768`) are separate types, each with its error enum, `agree`
  and `Calls` plus `delegate!`. The Helmholtz build (`helmholtz/fmm.rs:507-690`) has no
  work-weighted cut, near rule, overlap, table cache, kind, stage or build timings, and no
  device. ✔ It computes `split_shared` (`helmholtz/fmm.rs:531`) and then calls
  `Evaluator::new` (`:657`), which takes every rank as its own node; Laplace passes the node
  to `Evaluator::with_routing` (`fmm.rs:2131`). The scale-out machinery of `fmm.rs` is
  written against `LaplaceOperator`, `Engine` and `CostModel`, so parity as things stand
  means restating steps of the 365-line `FmmBuilder::build`.
- **L2 (medium). The plane-wave M2L level call exists twice.**
  `fmm-exec/src/operator_plane_wave.rs` (361 lines) with `operator.rs:1634-1717`, against
  `fmm-exec/src/helmholtz/operator_plane_wave.rs` (694 lines): `SLOT_OF`, `NO_SLOT`,
  `NO_BOX` and `sources_by_slot` are the same statements; the parent runs, the
  `by_child`/`parents` split and the loop over vectors and frames are restated. They differ
  in the inner arithmetic and Helmholtz's chunked buffer, and have drifted: Laplace splits
  by child at a quarter (`PLANE_CHILD_SHARE = 4`, `operator.rs:549`), Helmholtz at a half.
  The device path restates the order a third time (`device.rs:2689-2998`).
- **L3 (low). `TableCache` covers Laplace's families only;** the Helmholtz tables are
  rebuilt on every run. A gap, not a duplication.
- **L4 (low). Names and descriptions still say "Laplace".** `fmm-exec/src/lib.rs:1-11` and
  `:86`; the `Cargo.toml` descriptions of fmm-ref, fmm-tables and fmm-exec;
  `fmm-kernels/src/plane_wave.rs:1` is tagged "Helmholtz CH7.5a" and is Laplace's.
