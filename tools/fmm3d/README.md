# tools/fmm3d: the FMM3D comparison (Phase 6 T9, C6.8)

The scripts that compare our FMM with [FMM3D](https://github.com/flatironinstitute/FMM3D)
(analytic Laplace, the plan's one external baseline: laplace-fmm-plan §8.3, decision 9 of
docs/phase4/README.md) at matched accuracy, on the same problems and the same machine,
one node: docs/phase6/T9-fmm3d-comparison.md, designed in docs/design/optimisation.md
§8.1. The report is `fmm-validate/results/phase6-fmm3d.md`.

FMM3D is built and run **outside the workspace**: no crate links it or depends on it, and
nothing of it is committed (its sources, builds and outputs stay under `FMM3D_ROOT`).

| File | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `build.sh` | fetches FMM3D at the pinned release (v2.1.0, the commit and the tarball's SHA-256 checked), builds it with OpenMP with the machine's compilers in two variants, and links the driver against each |
| `driver.f90` | the FMM3D side: reads a problem file, checks FMM3D's normalisation, times FMM3D's setup and its calls, writes φ and ∇φ at the sampled targets |
| `run.sh` | the steps of a machine's comparison: `problems`, `sweep`, `match`, `time`, and `crosscheck` (the two codes' outputs against each other at every point) |
| `helmholtz.py` | Helmholtz CH1.4: the driver's Helmholtz modes from Python (problem and output files, the two-point check and the identification of FMM3D's conventions, the error norms) and the R12 sweep; Python 3.9 standard library only |

Our side is the sweep `t9-fmm3d` of `tools/scaling/run.sh` (and `t9-device`, our CUDA
path), on the same problems.

## The method

- **Problems**: the cube and the Plummer sphere of `nd_fmm_validate::scaling::Workload`
  (every point and charge generated by index from the `scaling` example's seed) at
  N = 10⁵, 10⁶ and 10⁷, sources equal targets, f64. `nd-fmm-bench --write-problem` writes
  each to one binary file (`fmm-bench/src/problem.rs`): the points, 8 charge vectors, the
  1,000 sampled targets of the `scaling` harness's errors (`Workload::sample`), and the
  direct sum there, `nd_fmm_ref::p2p::direct_sum` in f64 divided by 4π (the potential of
  CONVENTIONS §3.1).
- **Errors**: both codes against that direct sum at those targets, with the `scaling`
  harness's metrics (relative L2 and max of φ and of ∇φ per vector, the root mean square
  over the 8 vectors). Ours from `scaling --errors 8`; FMM3D's from its outputs, measured by
  `nd-fmm-bench --check-output`.
- **The normalisation**: CONVENTIONS §3.1 takes φ = q / (4π r). FMM3D up to v2.0.0
  computed q / r ("We use (1/r) for the Green's function, without the 1/(4\pi)
  scaling"); **v2.1.0 computes q / (4π r)** (its `lfmm3d.f`: "We use 1/(4\pi)*(1/r) for
  the Green's function"). The driver checks it on two unit charges at distance 1 before anything else
  and scales by what it finds (1 for v2.1.0), stopping on anything else.
- **Matched accuracy**: for each of our p ∈ {3, 6, 8, 12}, FMM3D's `eps` whose relative
  L2 error of φ is closest to ours (in its logarithm), over the grid of `run.sh sweep`
  (1-2-5 per decade, 5 × 10⁻¹ … 10⁻¹⁰), per problem and variant; both errors reported.
  FMM3D's error moves in steps (its expansion order and its plane-wave quadratures are
  chosen from `eps` by tables), so several `eps` give the same error and a match is
  only as close as those steps allow.
- **Precomputation apart**: ours, the build (tree, plan, tables) apart from the
  evaluation (the `scaling` report's build by part). FMM3D keeps nothing between calls,
  so every call does its setup again; the driver times that setup once on its own (FMM3D's
  `lndiv`, `pts_tree_mem`, `pts_tree_build`, `pts_tree_sort`, called as `lfmm3d` calls
  them) and the calls whole.
- **Threads**: one thread, and every core of the node: ours with Phase 5N's split of
  ranks × threads (docs/design/node-m2l.md §7: 1 × 12 on the M3 Max, 4 × 10 on Kathleen,
  8 × 9 on locust; `T9_SPLIT`), FMM3D with OpenMP over the same cores
  (`OMP_NUM_THREADS`), its defaults otherwise.
- **Several vectors**: FMM3D's vectorised interface (`lfmm3d_s_c_g_vec`, 8 densities in
  one call) beside ours with `--rhs 8` (`Fmm::evaluate_many`, Phase 6 T5).
- **The outputs against each other** (`run.sh crosscheck`, on Kathleen at N = 10⁵ and
  10⁶): ours at every p (`nd-fmm-bench --write-output`) and FMM3D at the matched `eps`
  (the driver's 7th argument, `FULL` = 1) write φ and ∇φ at every point;
  `nd-fmm-bench --compare` measures each FMM3D build against ours at its p over all N
  points, and `--check-output` each output against the direct sum at the sampled targets
  (the same errors as above, from these files).
- **Our configuration**: the batched host M2L of Phase 6 T2 (`--strategy batched`), with
  and without the opt-in fused multiply–add (`--mul-add fused`), the P2P kernel `auto`
  except on Kathleen, where AVX-512 (Phase 6 T6, opt-in) is named (`T9_P2P=avx512`).

## Building

```sh
FMM3D_ROOT=/path/outside/the/repo tools/fmm3d/build.sh           # fetch and build
FMM3D_ROOT=... tools/fmm3d/build.sh test                         # FMM3D's own tests
```

Two variants, each in `$FMM3D_ROOT/<variant>/` (`lib/libfmm3d.a`, `bin/fmm3d_driver`,
`build-info.txt` with the compilers and the compile lines, `build.log`):

- `default`: `make lib`, FMM3D's "easy-to-install version", with OpenMP (on by default);
- `fastker`: `make lib FAST_KER=ON`, its "high-performance mode" (vectorised C++ direct
  kernels and its larger leaves).

Compilers: on macOS Homebrew's GCC (`gfortran`, `gcc-16`, `g++-16`) with FMM3D's
`make.inc.macos_arm64.gnu`; on Linux the machine's `gfortran`, `gcc` and `g++` with
FMM3D's Linux defaults (`-O3 -march=native -funroll-loops`): on Kathleen the gcc 12.3.0
module of tools/kathleen/env.sh, on locust the system's GCC 11.4.1. `FC`, `CC` and `CXX`
override them.

## Running, on one machine

```sh
export FMM3D_ROOT=/path/outside/the/repo OUT=bench-results/fmm3d-<machine>
tools/fmm3d/run.sh problems sweep                     # problem files; FMM3D's errors per eps
OUT=bench-results/t9-<machine> tools/scaling/run.sh t9-fmm3d    # ours (unsandboxed on macOS)
OURS=bench-results/t9-<machine> tools/fmm3d/run.sh match time   # matched eps; FMM3D timed
```

On the M3 Max the timed steps run outside the Claude Code sandbox, which blocks `ps` (the
quiet-machine gate) and `sysctl` (the CPU's name). On locust, check the load first and
state it (docs/phase4s/README.md, "Timing"); `t9-device` (`DEVICE=cuda`) needs the H100
free of other users' jobs.

## Kathleen

Builds and runs are jobs (tools/kathleen/README.md, "The login-node rule"); only the
download runs on the login node:

```sh
tools/kathleen/sync.sh
tools/kathleen/remote.sh 'tools/fmm3d/build.sh fetch'
tools/kathleen/submit.sh --time=01:30:00 -- fmm3d.sbatch build problems
tools/kathleen/submit.sh fmm3d.sbatch sweep
tools/kathleen/submit.sh '--export=ALL,T9_P2P=avx512,TIMEOUT=7200' -- sweep.sbatch t9-fmm3d
tools/kathleen/submit.sh '--export=ALL,OURS=/scratch/scratch/ucahtbe/fmm/logs/scaling-<id>' -- fmm3d.sbatch match time
tools/kathleen/submit.sh --time=02:00:00 -- fmm3d.sbatch build crosscheck
```

`fmm3d.sbatch` keeps FMM3D in `$FMM_KATHLEEN_ROOT/fmm3d` and writes the launches to the
root's `logs/fmm3d/`; `sweep.sbatch` writes ours to `logs/scaling-<job id>/`. A
`singlenode` job runs at most 6 hours: split our side with `ONLY` (for example
`ONLY='n10000000-.*r1x1'` for the one-thread runs at N = 10⁷, `ONLY='n(100000|1000000)-|N1-'`
for the rest).

## Helmholtz (CH1.4)

The driver has two Helmholtz modes beside the Laplace usage above (`driver.f90`'s header
has the file formats); `tools/fixtures/gen_helmholtz_fmm3d.py` makes `nd-fmm-ref`'s FMM3D
fixtures with them (tools/fixtures/README.md). Report:
`fmm-validate/results/helmholtz-ch1-4.md`.

- `fmm3d_driver helmholtz-check`: the two-point check of proposal §9.2 against
  CONVENTIONS §3.15, "Kernel and factors" (drafted in CH1.4, adopted in CH1.1). It runs
  `hfmm3d_s_c_g` and `h3ddirectcg` for real, complex and imaginary k and stops unless
  both compute e^{ikr}/(4πr) and its gradient in the target. Every `helmholtz` run does
  this check first, as the Laplace runs check the normalisation.
- `fmm3d_driver helmholtz PROBLEM PREFIX ZK_RE ZK_IM direct,EPS,... [REPEAT]`: FMM3D's
  direct sum (`h3ddirectcg`) or its FMM (`hfmm3d_s_c_g`, `hfmm3d_st_c_g`) at the
  problem's sampled sources and targets.

```sh
tools/fmm3d/build.sh driver                         # relink the driver after a change
python3 tools/fmm3d/helmholtz.py check              # the check and the identification
OMP_NUM_THREADS=12 python3 tools/fmm3d/helmholtz.py r12 --n 100000   # R12, a Markdown table
```

`r12` measures `hfmm3d` against `h3ddirectcg` at 1,000 sampled sources (the relative ℓ2
of φ and ∇φ, proposal §9.1) on the unit cube and a sphere surface. It covers imaginary,
real and complex k over kD and eps 10⁻³ to 10⁻¹². On Kathleen the same runs as job steps:

```sh
tools/kathleen/submit.sh --time=03:00:00 -- fmm3d.sbatch driver helmholtz-check helmholtz-fixtures r12
```
