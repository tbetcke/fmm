# Phase 5 code on Kathleen: one node, two nodes, the x86 host (Phase 5N T2, C5N.2)

The distributed host FMM of Phase 5, unchanged, on Kathleen (UCL's Slurm cluster, Intel
Xeon Gold 6248, Omni-Path): one node at 1–40 ranks, the ranks × threads splits and the
M2L strategies; the first inter-node figures at 1 and 2 nodes (strong scaling at
N = 10⁶ and 10⁷, weak scaling at 10⁶ points per node), the exchanges against
distributed-fmm.md §8.2's network model, overlap, the replicated data and memory up to
80 ranks, the errors; and the x86 host on one rank, the AVX2 + FMA P2P kernels timed on
Intel for the first time (docs/phase5n/T2-kathleen-baseline.md). Run on 2026-10-10,
17:09–20:18, in 8 jobs (§13).

**Every figure below is measured on Kathleen**, in Slurm jobs on whole nodes (nodes are
exclusive, so there is no load check), named nodes × ranks per node × threads per rank,
with `mpirun` inside the allocation (decision 4), every rank bound to its cores, Open MPI
4.1.6 over PSM2 (Omni-Path) between and within the nodes, release builds and every BLAS
variable at 1. The memory formula of distributed-fmm.md §9.2 and the exchange model of
§8.2 are labelled *model*, and so is anything derived from them. **Two nodes (80 ranks)
are the most measured**: no job here is larger (README, "Working on Kathleen"), and no
figure says anything measured about thousands of ranks.

**Not measured:**
- **More than 2 nodes**: the user's cap of 2 nodes per job (from 2026-10-10). 4 nodes
  and beyond are "not measured" in this phase.
- **AVX-512**: the CPU has it, `nd-fmm-simd` has no AVX-512 path (deferred, simd-p2p.md
  §4.7); every P2P here is AVX2 + FMA.
- **Cache or frequency counters**: none were read. Where a cause (the cache pressure of
  the dense tables, the all-core clock) is named, it is inferred from the timings, not
  measured.
- **`srun` as the launcher of the sweeps**: decision 4 chose `mpirun`; T1 measured both on
  2 nodes, and the sweeps did not repeat that comparison under load.
- **Redistribution and load-balance sweeps** (T10's `redistribution`, `balance`) and
  N = 10⁵: not asked for by the brief on Kathleen; the per-rank work and imbalance of every
  run are in the raw files.
